iPhone Installation Guide

  1. Open your deployed website URL in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button (the box with an upward arrow) at the bottom bar.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Launch VaultTools right from your home screen like a native app!
